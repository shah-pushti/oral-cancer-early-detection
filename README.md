# Oral cancer early detection: the evidence at each step, from risk to coverage

A person passes through five steps on the way from risk to treatment: **risk, screening, referral, diagnosis, coverage**. This repository collects the published result at each step, the limits of that result, and the open question that follows from it. Every figure links to its source.

> General information only. It does not replace an examination by a dentist or doctor.

## 1. Risk: who is at risk of oral cancer

| Result | Source |
|---|---|
| GLOBOCAN 2022 estimates 389,846 new cases of lip and oral cavity cancer and 188,438 deaths worldwide. India: an estimated 143,759 cases and 79,979 deaths. | [World Cancer Research Fund, GLOBOCAN 2022](https://www.wcrf.org/preventing-cancer/cancer-statistics/mouth-and-oral-cancer-statistics/) |
| Highest age-standardised incidence: Papua New Guinea 17.5 per 100,000, Bangladesh 10.3, India 9.9, Pakistan 9.2, Sri Lanka 8.1. World rate 4.0. | [World Cancer Research Fund, GLOBOCAN 2022](https://www.wcrf.org/preventing-cancer/cancer-statistics/mouth-and-oral-cancer-statistics/) |
| Approximately 70 to 80 percent of oral cancer cases in India are attributable to tobacco and areca nut use (the authors cite earlier research). | [Francis and Reddy, ecancermedicalscience, 2026](https://ecancer.org/en/journal/article/2162-oral-cancer-burden-and-mortality-in-india-a-globocan-2022-analysis-with-regional-comparison) |

**Limit:** case counts depend on population size; incidence rates are estimates where registries are incomplete.

## 2. Screening: examination of people without symptoms

| Result | Source |
|---|---|
| Kerala cluster-randomised trial, 13 clusters, 1996 to 2004. Among people who used tobacco, alcohol or both, the oral cancer mortality rate ratio (intervention versus control) was 0.66 (95% CI 0.45 to 0.95). In the whole study population the difference was not statistically significant. | [Sankaranarayanan et al., The Lancet, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(05)66658-5) |
| Study description by the trial's research group. | [IARC Screening Group](https://screening.iarc.fr/oral_cancer.php) |
| A Cochrane review included the Kerala trial as the only randomised trial of oral cancer screening and rated it at high risk of bias. | [Brocklehurst et al., Cochrane 2013, CD004150](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004150.pub4) |

**Limit:** one trial, in one region where tobacco chewing is common. It does not show whether the result holds where incidence is lower.

## 3. Referral: from a positive screening result to a specialist

| Result | Source |
|---|---|
| 5,145 people screened positive and 3,218 (63 percent) complied with referral. | [Sankaranarayanan et al., The Lancet, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(05)66658-5) |
| Cancer Research UK on urgent referral for suspected mouth cancer. | [Cancer Research UK](https://www.cancerresearchuk.org/about-cancer/mouth-cancer/getting-diagnosed/referral-specialist) |

**Open question:** in workplace screening of adults who use tobacco, how many oral potentially malignant disorders are detected, and what proportion of screen-positive participants attend the referral?

## 4. Diagnosis: the stage at which the cancer is found

| Result | Source |
|---|---|
| Invasive cancer of the oral cavity and pharynx, SEER 18 registries (United States), diagnoses 2007 to 2013: 30 percent localised, 47 percent regional, 19 percent distant, 4 percent unstaged. Five-year relative survival: 83.7 percent localised, 64.2 percent regional, 38.5 percent distant. | [SEER Cancer Statistics Review 1975 to 2014, Table 20.10](https://seer.cancer.gov/archive/csr/1975_2014/results_single/sect_20_table.10.pdf) |
| Germany, lip, oral cavity and pharynx: five-year relative survival 53 percent in men and 63 percent in women (2015 to 2017). The stage distribution at diagnosis did not change between 1999 and 2017. | [Jansen et al., Bundesgesundheitsblatt, 2021](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8316202/) |
| Robert Koch Institute estimate: about 13,900 new cases in Germany for 2016 (as reported). | [zm-online](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/fallzahlen-fuer-mundhoehlen-und-rachenkrebs-sinken-gegen-den-trend) |
| Symptoms for which the NHS advises seeing a GP or dentist. | [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/mouth-cancer/symptoms) · [NHS Wales](https://cavuhb.nhs.wales/news/latest-news/mouth-cancer-the-key-signs-symptoms-and-causes) |

**Limit:** survival by stage does not show the effect of earlier detection (lead-time bias). Both registries combine the oral cavity with the pharynx.

**Open question:** what proportion of oral cavity cancers are first suspected at a dental visit, and does the stage at diagnosis differ between patients with and without a dental visit in the preceding year?
